Job Search Agent is a personal educational and portfolio project. It helps organize opportunities; it is not a recruiter or an employment service.

Review before acting

Postings may change or close. Scores are estimates, and sponsorship classifications express uncertainty rather than employer commitments. Verify job requirements, compensation, eligibility, sponsorship, and application links with the employer. No employment or sponsorship outcome is guaranteed.

Users choose their preferences, supply only resumes they are authorized to use, authorize any Google account connection, and decide whether to apply. The program does not apply automatically. Protect local credentials and use job sources in accordance with their access rules.

The project is provided as available. External APIs and email services can fail or change, and automated reports may be delayed or unavailable. Review the Privacy Policy for data handling.
